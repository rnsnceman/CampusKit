## Expectations

## Missed commitments and conflict process
For a first missed commitment, discuss the cause and agree on a recovery task and deadline. Record the agreement on the board. Repeated or material non-participation requires a documented team discussion and written notice to the member with an opportunity to respond. If unresolved, present factual records to the professor and request guidance. The course instructions allow an unproductive flag and a zero grade for affected releases until the flag is removed; grading and flag decisions remain with the professor. Team members do not unilaterally impose grades.

Membership stays fixed for the project. A requested move because of irreconcilable conflict needs the professor's approval and updated contracts for both affected teams.

## Agreement
By signing the completed contract, each member accepts the agreed work, communication expectations, and escalation process. The team will revise the contract together if scope or professor-approved membership changes.
